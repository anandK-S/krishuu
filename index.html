1:11 Dil Se 12 October

Happy Birthday, krishnaa | krishuu | 1:11! ❤️🌷

Pata hai, aaj tere baare mein kuchh likhne baitha hoon, par samajh nahi aa raha ki shuruaat kahan se karun. Kuchh logon ke baare mein likhna aasan hota hai, par kuchh log aise hote hain jinhe kuchh shabdon mein samjhana thoda mushkil hota hai. Tu shayad unhi mein se ek hai.

Mujhe nahi pata tu khud ko kis nazar se dekhti hai, par main chahta hoon ki kabhi tu khud ko un nazron se bhi dekhe jinse tere aas-paas ke log tujhe dekhte hain. Shayad tab tujhe samajh aaye ki teri chhoti-chhoti baatein, tera apna andaaz aur tera hona bhi kisi ke din ko achha bana sakta hai.

Main samajhta hoon ki tera dil us insaan ke liye kuchh mehsoos karta hai, jise tune apne dil mein ek khaas jagah di hai. Aur main jaanta hoon ki shayad main tujhe woh khushi na de sakun, jo tu uske saath sochti hai. Kabhi-kabhi toh lagta hai, Bhagwan se agar ek ichha maangne ka mauka mile, toh bas itna maangunga ki zindagi ke us mod par mujhe thoda rok dete, jab tum ke baare mein sochna shuru kiya tha… shayad kuchh baatein dil aur dimag mein aane se pehle hi reh jaati.

Par ab jo hai, woh bhi zindagi ka ek hissa hai. Aage kya likha hai, ye toh Bhagwan hi jaanta hai. Main kal ke baare mein sochkar aaj ko uljhana nahi chahta. Bas itna jaanta hoon ki tu mere liye khaas hai, aur tere liye jo achha kar sakta hoon, woh dil se karna chahta hoon.

“

Kuchh baatein kismat par chhod dena hi achha hota hai, par kisi ke liye dil se achha chahna toh aaj bhi humare haath mein hai. ❤️

Aur agar kabhi tujhe lage ki duniya tujhe samajh nahi paa rahi, toh ek baar mujhse baat kar lena. Ho sakta hai main har baat ka hal na de paun, ya har baar tere mann ki baat samajh na paun, par main teri baat sunne ki koshish zaroor karunga. Tujhe har baar khud ko samjhana pade, aisa zaroori nahi. Kabhi-kabhi bas kisi ka saath hona bhi kaafi hota hai. Aur yaar, jab bhi tujhe aise kisi saath ki zaroorat ho, main hoon. ❤️

Tere birthday par meri bas yahi wish hai ki teri zindagi mein khushiyan kabhi kam na hon. Jab cheezein tere hisaab se na hon, tab bhi tujhe khud par bharosa rahe. Tere sapne poore hon, tujhe aise log milen jo teri kadar karein, aur tu kabhi khud ko kisi se kam na samjhe.

Main chahta hoon ki jab tu kuchh saalon baad apni zindagi ke in dino ko yaad kare, toh tere chehre par muskurahat ho aur dil mein achhi yaadein.

Aur haan, zindagi mein chahe kitne bhi naye log milen aur kitne hi naye mod aayein, meri wish hamesha yahi rahegi ki tu khush rahe, apne sapne poore kare aur khud par hamesha garv kare.

“

Kuchh logon ke liye achha chahne ke liye kisi khaas wajah ki zaroorat nahi hoti. Bas unka khush rehna achha lagta hai. Tu mere liye unhi logon mein se ek hai. ❤️

Once again, Happy Birthday! 🌸

Apna khayal rakhna, yaar. Hamesha khush rehna aur apni woh smile kabhi mat khona jo tujhe, tu banati hai. ❤️
